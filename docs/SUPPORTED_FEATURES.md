# Supported features and known limits

The MVP supports ordinary single-column DOCX paragraphs, standard Heading 1–3, simple tables, hyperlinks and embedded images for a verified subset of formatting operations. Effective values cover document defaults, paragraph/character style inheritance and direct formatting. Theme fonts, complex numbering, toggle behavior and other unresolved cases remain UNKNOWN rather than inheriting an invented default.

Page preview is generated by LibreOffice, not Microsoft Word. pdfplumber text alignment is heuristic and reports LOCATED, AMBIGUOUS, UNLOCATED or NOT_RENDERED independently of static detection. Repeated short text without unique context is not highlighted. Complex table reading order, floating objects and unusual encodings can reduce coverage.

Track Changes, macros, OLE, encryption, digital signatures and document protection block dangerous automation. Equations, complex fields, text boxes and other protected targets require manual review. Shared global styles are not rewritten in MVP; local direct formatting is used only for approved targets.

Not included: OCR for scanned guidelines, arbitrary PDF editing, DOC/DOCM repair, full online Word editing, collaboration, accounts, billing, or a promise of identical Word/LibreOffice pagination.
